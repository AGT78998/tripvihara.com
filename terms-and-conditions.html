Please read our cancellation and refund policy and the conditions that apply when booking a Trip Vihara tour.

1. Cancellation & Refund Policy

A booking amount (20% of the total trip cost) is a non-refundable reservation fee for all Trip Vihara trips.

If you cancel your booking

  • 10 days or more before the trip date: Full refund, excluding the 20% reservation charge.
  • 5 to 9 days before the trip date: 50% of the amount, excluding the 20% reservation charge, is refunded.
  • Less than 5 days before the trip date: No refund.

If Trip Vihara cancels or reschedules a trip

If we cancel or reschedule a trip from our side, you may choose one of the following:

  1. Receive a full refund of everything you paid; or
  2. Choose any other tour, and the money will be adjusted toward it.

If a trip is cancelled due to circumstances beyond our control

If a tour is cancelled due to bad weather or other reasons beyond our control, the booking amount is retained and the rest is refunded, or you can move to another date and the money will be adjusted.
