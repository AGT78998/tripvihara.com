About Us

Aryabhatta gave some of mathematics's earliest concepts like Zero in Pataliputra. Ours is a smaller story: one person's belief that Bihar deserves to be seen differently.

Namaste everyone, My name is Subhav and I am the founder of Trip Vihara.

While working in corporate, I again and again found people surprised to learn that the histories of Nalanda, Vaishali, and Bodh Gaya originated here; that Buddhism, Jainism & Sikhism have deep roots here; and that some of the biggest empires of the past were ruled from here, stretching to the edges of the known world.

The more those conversations repeated, the more I realized the problem wasn't that Bihar's story was small. It was that almost nobody was telling it.

Some of the world's great heritage sites share a hard lesson: a monument can be famous for centuries while the town around it remains underdeveloped. So TripVihara works differently from the start. We work locally, with sites, people & customs.

We believe tourism should leave Bihar better than it found it — supporting local people whenever practical, respecting the communities that welcome us, and helping visitors understand Bihar rather than simply passing through it.

Bihar receives high number of tourists, especially from East Asian nations. Our aim is to develop the tourism ecosystem of Bihar in the best possible way, so that better memories for visitors as well as locals are created here. Needless to say, we thrive to provide best services possible.

We're still a young company, and we won't get everything right from day one. But these principles will guide every journey we design.

— Subhav Samarth, Founder, TripVihara

Guardian Support

Bihar's viharas once welcomed travelers with shelter and guidance, while Ashoka's Dhamma Mahamatras carried his message of care across the empire. Guardian Support carries that same spirit forward.

Every journey is different, but our support is built around these 10 considerations:

  • Itinerary planning & Packing advice
  • Travel guidance
  • Advice for local travel as per local customs and traditions
  • Good medical assistance
  • Language support
  • Local recommendations for basics like hygienic food, water, washroom
  • Digital payments
  • Connectivity help
  • Unexpected itinerary changes
  • On-ground problem-solving

Note: We do NOT charge any fees for any of these supports. You will directly pay the service provider. We will connect you to the best service provider according to your requirement.
